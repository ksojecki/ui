---
title: Dropdown
description: A button that opens a panel, usually with a menu.
group: navigation
components: [Dropdown]
---

The trigger is a [Button](../button/): style it with `triggerPurpose`, `triggerTone` and `triggerSize`. `align="end"` aligns the panel to the right edge. The panel closes on Escape and, with `closeOnSelect`, after a choice.
