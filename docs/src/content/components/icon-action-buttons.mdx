`EditButton`, `NewButton` and `DeleteButton` are [IconButton](../icon-button/)s with a fixed icon and tone, so lists in every app look the same. They take every IconButton prop except `icon`.

## Usage

With `tone="ghost"` the button switches to the solid variant, for use on coloured surfaces.
